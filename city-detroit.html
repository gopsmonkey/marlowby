Why Detroit

  • The Renaissance Center’s central tower is the tallest building in Michigan.
  • The Guardian Building, finished in 1929, is known as the “Cathedral of Finance” for its tiled interior.
  • Berry Gordy founded Motown Records in Detroit in 1959.
