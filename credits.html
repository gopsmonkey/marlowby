About this site

Marlowby is a fictional company, and this website was made for the Business Professionals of America (BPA) 365 Website Design and Development Team event, 2026–2027. The makers, products, test-box quotes and vote counts are illustrative, not real people or businesses. No stock photography or third-party images are used: every picture, map, illustration and 3D scene is drawn by code written for this project. The websites listed were studied for layout and market research only; no text, images, code or branding from them was copied.
