Tucson has been farmed for more than 4,000 years, and its food still comes from the desert: mesquite flour, prickly pear and tepary beans. The skies are dark enough that the city limits its lights for the observatories.

Why Tucson

  • In 2015 Tucson became the first city in the United States named a UNESCO City of Gastronomy.
  • Mission San Xavier del Bac was finished in 1797; its east tower never received a dome.
  • The saguaro cactus grows wild only in the Sonoran Desert, which surrounds the city.
