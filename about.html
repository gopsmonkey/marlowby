It started witha full trunk.

On a long road trip we kept buying things from small shops: pralines, a mug, a jar of chile. Back home, friends kept asking where to get them. Marlowby is our answer.

How we got here

  1. Summer 2025

    The trunk

    A road trip through 11 states. We came home with a trunk full of things we couldn’t buy anywhere else.

  2. Spring 2026

    Forty test boxes

    We packed Savannah boxes by hand and mailed them to friends of friends in 19 states.

  3. Fall 2026

    First makers

    Twelve Savannah shops said yes. Every one was paid before a single box was sold.

  4. Jan 1, 2027

    Box No. 01 ships

    The first city goes out to founding members. Santa Fe follows in February.

Four promises

To the people who make the goods, and to the people who open the box.

  1. Truly local

    Every item is made or sold by an independent business in that month’s city. No national brands, ever.

  2. Paid before we ship

    Makers get a fair wholesale price up front, with no exclusive contracts. They keep selling however they like.

  3. The story comes too

    Every item has a card with who made it and how, written with the maker, plus a link to reorder from them directly.

  4. Light on packaging

    Recyclable boxes, paper fill, no plastic tape and carbon-offset shipping.
