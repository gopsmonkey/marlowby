Local goods, the way they should be: Every item is made or sold by an independent business in that month’s city. No national brands., We pay makers a fair wholesale price before the box ships, so a small shop never waits on us., The field guide and maker cards are written with people who live there, not copied from travel sites..

How it works

  1. Pick a sizePostcard, Neighborhood or Grand Tour. Pay monthly, or yearly for 15% off.
  2. We go to the cityOur team spends weeks with local shops and buys their goods at fair prices, paid up front.
  3. Your box ships on the 1stThe city is revealed on the 20th of the month before. Skip any month by the 15th.
  4. Open it upEach box comes with a field guide, a fold-out map and the story behind every item.
