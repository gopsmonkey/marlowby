Frequently asked questions

What is Marlowby?

A monthly subscription box. Each month features a different U.S. city, with goods from its independent businesses, a field guide, a fold-out map and, on larger plans, an at-home experience.

Do I get to choose the city?

Everyone gets the same city each month, so the whole community explores together. Members vote on one city a year, and you can skip any month.

What if I have allergies or a special diet?

On Neighborhood and Grand Tour you can set a taste profile (vegan, vegetarian, gluten-free, nut-free, dairy-free or no caffeine). We swap food items for something suitable from the same city.

Will I ever get the same item twice?

No. Every month is a new city with new makers.

When will my box arrive?

Boxes ship on the 1st and arrive in 3 to 6 business days. We email a tracking link when yours leaves the warehouse.

Where do you ship?

Free to every address in the lower 48 states. Alaska and Hawaii are a flat $9. International shipping is not available yet.

What if something arrives broken?

Send us a photo within 14 days and we will replace or refund that item.

How do I skip a month or pause?

Choose Skip or Pause in your account before the 15th. You can pause for up to three months at a time, and it never costs anything.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel a monthly plan before the 15th to stop the next box. Yearly plans are refunded for any boxes that have not shipped.

Can I change my plan?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade before the 15th and the change starts with your next box.

How do gift subscriptions work?

Choose 3, 6 or 12 months and pay once. The first box arrives gift-wrapped with your note. Gift plans never renew on their own.

Can a gift start on a certain date?

Yes. Pick a start month at checkout and we will email the person a digital gift card on the day you choose.
