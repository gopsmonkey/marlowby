A city, packedinto a box.

Here is what happens between a city and your front door, and what you will find when you open the lid.

1

Pick a size

Postcard, Neighborhood or Grand Tour. Pay monthly, or yearly and save 15%.

2

We go to the city

We spend weeks meeting shop owners and buy their goods at fair prices, paid up front.

3

It ships on the 1st

Free shipping in the lower 48. Arrives in 3 to 6 business days, with tracking.

4

Open it up

Taste it, use it, read the stories, then plan a trip if you fall for the place.

What’s inside

The goods change every month. These five things are in every box.

  • Local goods

    4 to 12 items made or sold by independent businesses in that city: food, home goods, art.

  • The field guide

    A small booklet on the city’s history, neighborhoods and food, written with locals.

  • A fold-out map

    Every shop in the box is marked, plus the places locals say not to miss.

  • Maker cards

    One card per item: who made it, how, and where to reorder.

  • An experience

    In Neighborhood and Grand Tour: a recipe, playlist, craft or live workshop from the city.
